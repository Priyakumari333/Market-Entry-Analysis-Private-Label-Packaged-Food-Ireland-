# 🛒 FMCG Market Entry Analysis- Irish Private Label Packaged Food Market

**Tool:** Power BI Desktop  
**Author:** Priya Kumari  
**Domain:** Commercial Strategy | Market Analysis | Financial Modelling  
**Status:** Completed

---

## 📌 Business Problem

> *Should an FMCG company enter the Irish private label packaged food market and if yes, at what price point to ensure at least 30% gross margin while remaining competitive?*

A strategic go/no-go decision requiring competitive pricing analysis, demand forecasting, cost structure modelling, and margin optimisation across 5 product lines.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Determine market entry viability for the Irish private label packaged food segment
- Identify optimal price points to achieve minimum 30% gross margin
- Analyse urban vs rural demand distribution across product categories
- Benchmark target prices against competitor pricing
- Assess logistics risk and its impact on profitability
- Deliver a phased launch recommendation with a clear timeline

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Product Lines | 5 (Biscuits, Potato Chips, Label Noodles, Private Label Pasta, Private Label Cornflakes) |
| Markets | Urban (Dublin, Cork) and Rural Ireland |
| Total Monthly Demand | ~50,000 units |
| Total Revenue Modelled | €79,040 |
| Cost Components | Raw Materials, Packaging, Logistics, Fixed Costs |

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Average Gross Margin | 30.25% (above 30% target ✅) |
| Urban Demand Share | 69.72% |
| Rural Demand Share | 30.28% |
| Products meeting 30% margin threshold | 3 out of 5 |
| Target price vs competitor price | 5–10% lower across all products |

**Highest demand product:** Biscuits (13,000 units/month) but below margin threshold, requiring cost review before launch.

**Highest margin product:** Private Label Cornflakes (32.5% gross margin).

**Primary risk identified:** Logistics costs (8–9% of revenue) show highest variability and directly reduce gross margin at scale.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendation

**Go decision — selective, phased urban launch.**

| Product | Monthly Demand | Margin Status | Launch Decision | Launch Price |
|---------|---------------|---------------|-----------------|--------------|
| Biscuits | 13,000 | Below Target | Review Cost | €1.25 |
| Potato Chips | 11,500 | Above Target ✅ | Launch | €1.45 |
| Label Noodles | 8,500 | Above Target ✅ | Launch | €1.65 |
| Private Label Pasta | 7,700 | Below Target | Review Cost | €1.70 |
| Private Label Cornflakes | 9,500 | Above Target ✅ | Launch | €2.00 |

**Timeline:**
- Months 1–3: Urban launch (3 products — Potato Chips, Label Noodles, Cornflakes)
- Month 6: Performance review
- Month 12: Break-even target + rural expansion decision
- Month 13: Cost review for Biscuits and Pasta
